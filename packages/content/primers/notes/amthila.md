# al-Amthila al-mukhtalifa (Emsile): transcription notes

## Source

- **Print**: an Ottoman lithograph of *Majmūʿat al-ṣarf* (Mecmûʿatü's-ṣarf: Marāḥ al-arwāḥ and al-ʿIzzī first, then al-Maqṣūd
  pp. 178–209, Bināʾ al-afʿāl pp. 210–235, al-Amthila pp. 236–256), each page with the
  matn in a frame, Ottoman Turkish explanations under the forms, a commentary in the margin and notes at the foot.
  The colophon at the end of al-Amthila (p. 256) reads: «تَمَّ الْكِتَابُ بِعَوْنِ الْمَلِكِ الْوَهَّابِ، حَرَّرَهُ الْفَقِيرُ حَسَن شَوْقِي
  بْنُ عُثْمَانَ الْوَهْبِيُّ …» with the date **1317** (1899–1900). The place is not printed on the pages we have
  (probably Istanbul); the printer is not named. Public domain.
- **Scan**: https://archive.org/details/mecmuatussarf (PDF `mecmuatussarf.pdf`, 267 pages): a modern facsimile reprint
  titled «كتب الصرف» with a new typeset table of contents (PDF pp. 2–6) and the lithograph's pages bound back to
  front: p. 256 = PDF p. 11, p. 236 = PDF p. 31. Only the lithograph's pages are used. The same lithograph's
  al-Amthila (pp. 236–256) is also on archive.org as https://archive.org/details/emsile_kitabi (lower resolution).
- The same scan serves bina, maqsud, izzi and marah.

## How it was checked

- Read from the page images (300 ppi masks rendered at 150 dpi), page by page; no digital text used. Every form of
  the print is vowelled; doubtful cells were cropped and enlarged (the plurals نَصَرَاتٌ p. 240 and p. 253, نِصَرَاتٌ
  p. 253, نُصَّارٌ p. 240).
- A script checked full vocalisation and the passage ids.

## How the tables are given

- One passage per table (the print's heading = the section heading); one row of the print's table per line, the
  forms of a row in the print's order (right to left) separated by «، »; a row that runs onto the next page is kept
  as the print breaks it, and a short row at the foot of a page stays short.
- Left out: the Ottoman Turkish under each form (e.g. «فعل ماضى بناء معلوم مفرد مذكر غائب معناسى …»), the marginal
  commentary (two different ones: pp. 236–246 and pp. 248–256), the notes at the foot of each page, the title
  «كتاب أمثلة» is kept as the first heading (vowels ours), the basmala is the first line of p1.
- Headings are vowelled by us (the print leaves them mostly bare): الْأَمْثِلَةُ الْمُطَّرِدَةُ مِنْ …; «الْغَيْرِ الْمِيمِيِّ»
  keeps the print's article on غير.
- The imperative is written اُنْصُرْ (alif with ḍamma), as the print writes it.

## Features of this print (kept as printed)

- al-Amthila al-mukhtalifa (pp. 236–237) gives only active forms, one per cell: نَصَرَ يَنْصُرُ نَصْرًا فَهُوَ نَاصِرٌ وَذَاكَ
  مَنْصُورٌ لَمْ يَنْصُرْ لَمَّا يَنْصُرْ مَا يَنْصُرُ لَا يَنْصُرُ لَنْ يَنْصُرَ لِيَنْصُرْ لَا يَنْصُرْ اُنْصُرْ لَا تَنْصُرْ مَنْصَرٌ مِنْصَرٌ
  نَصْرَةٌ نِصْرَةٌ نُصَيْرٌ نَصْرِيٌّ نَصَّارٌ أَنْصَرُ مَا أَنْصَرَهُ وَأَنْصِرْ بِهِ (no passive forms, no الظَّرْفُ مِنْهُ … lines
  found in other copies).
- The active amr and nahy of the absent (pp. 249–250) have six forms (no first person); the passive ones have
  eight, ending with لِأُنْصَرْ لِنُنْصَرْ / لَا أُنْصَرْ لَا نُنْصَرْ; the passive amr of the present (p. 251) again ends
  with لِأُنْصَرْ لِنُنْصَرْ.
- Ism al-fāʿil (p. 240): «نَاصِرٌ، نَاصِرَانِ، نَاصِرُونَ، نُصَّارٌ / وَنُصَّرٌ، وَنَصَرَةٌ، نَاصِرَةٌ، نَاصِرَتَانِ / نَاصِرَاتٌ،
  وَنَوَاصِرُ» — the wāw before some broken plurals is the print's.
- Taṣghīr (p. 253) has three forms only; ism al-tafḍīl (pp. 254–255) ends «نُصْرَيَاتٌ، وَنُصَرٌ».

## Uncertain readings

- None of substance. The vowel on the ṣād of the plural نَصَرَاتٌ (maṣdar, p. 240; marra, p. 253) and نِصَرَاتٌ
  (nawʿ, p. 253) is a small fatḥa in the lithograph, read enlarged.

## Sections

36 tables (s1–s36), 37 passages (s1, al-Amthila al-mukhtalifa, is split at the page: p1 p. 236, p2 p. 237).
