# Bināʾ al-afʿāl (Binâ): transcription notes

## Source

- **Print**: the same Ottoman lithograph of *Majmūʿat al-ṣarf* as `amthila` (see amthila/NOTES.md), here
  «هذا كتاب البناء في الصرف», pp. 210–235, the matn in a frame with dense interlinear and marginal glosses (a
  commentary, often signed «شرح»). The Bināʾ ends on p. 235 with the scribe's date **١٣١٦** (1898–99); the Amthila
  that follows is dated 1317. Public domain.
- **Scan**: https://archive.org/details/mecmuatussarf (PDF pp. 32–57 = pp. 235–210; the reprint binds the pages back
  to front, PDF page = 267 − print page).

## How it was checked

- Read from the page images at 150 dpi (doubtful words at 300 dpi), only the large script in the frame; the small
  glosses between the lines and in the margins are commentary and left out. No digital text was used.
- **Vocalisation**: the print vowels the paradigms (فَعَلَ يَفْعُلُ …, the mawzūn forms, many examples) and a few
  other words; we completed the rest. Script check: every consonant vowelled; ids p1–p46.
- The print has no headings except the title; our section headings are the print's own words that open each part
  («وَاثْنَا عَشَرَ بَابًا مِنْهَا لِمَا زَادَ عَلَى الثُّلَاثِيِّ», «النَّوْعُ الثَّانِي», «وَسِتَّةٌ لِلْمُلْحَقِ بِدَحْرَجَ» …), and
  for the last two parts the names the print gives them («الْأَقْسَامُ الثَّمَانِيَةُ», «أَقْسَامُ السَّبْعَةِ»). The
  opening basmala is in p1.
- Orthography normalised: المسئلة → الْمَسْأَلَةَ; سئل → سَأَلَ; ثلثون → ثَلَاثُونَ; the print's ى/ي is followed in
  غَزَى (sic), رَمَى, طَوَى, وَقَى and قوى (vowelled قَوِيَ).

## Readings kept as printed (worth a teacher's look)

- p. 211 and later: the print alternates «الْمُضَارِعِ» and «الْغَابِرِ» for the present tense; kept.
- p. 213 (p8): the lāzim example of bāb VI is «وَرِثَ زَيْدٌ» (clear in the print).
- p. 218–219 (p16): tafāʿala: «تَبَاعَدَ زَيْدٌ عَمْرًا» and «تَصَالَحَ الْقَوْمُ قَوْمًا» — the print has the accusatives
  (most texts: تَبَاعَدَ زَيْدٌ وَعَمْرٌو / تَصَالَحَ الْقَوْمُ).
- p. 220 (p18): «عَشُبَ الْأَرْضُ … اعْشَوْشَبَ الْأَرْضُ» with masculine verbs, as printed; the vowel on the shīn of
  عشب looks like a ḍamma (the dictionaries also give عَشِبَ).
- p. 221 (p19): «إِذَا سَارَ سَيْرًا سُرْعَةً فِي الْجُمْلَةِ» as printed (one would expect سَيْرًا سَرِيعًا).
- p. 222 (p21): the lāzim example of the quadriliteral reads «دربح/دربج» in the scan with the dot unclear; the gloss
  above it explains «أَيْ تَذَلَّلَ», which is the meaning of **دَرْبَخَ**, so we write دَرْبَخَ زَيْدٌ.
- p. 220 (p17): «نَحْوُ: أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ» — unvowelled in the print; we read the first person (the formula); the
  perfect اسْتَغْفَرَ is also possible.
- p. 227 (p31): «نَحْوُ: جَلْبَبَ زَيْدٌ فَتَجَلْبَبَ فِي ذَلِكَ الْجِلْبَابِ» — the vowel on the first verb is unclear; the
  passive جُلْبِبَ زَيْدٌ («Zayd was dressed in a jilbāb, and he put on that jilbāb») gives better sense. Check.
- p. 231 (p40): «وَهِيَ الْوَاوُ وَالْيَاءُ وَالْأَلِفُ، وَالْهَمْزَةُ وَالتَّضْعِيفُ» — hamza and doubling are named with the
  weak letters as what the ṣaḥīḥ verb lacks; punctuation ours.
- p. 234–235 (p44): «وَيُقَالُ لِهَذِهِ الْأَقْسَامِ: أَقْسَامُ السَّبْعَةِ» without the article on أقسام, as printed.
- p. 235 (p45): the closing verse is **Persian**, as printed: «صحيحست مثالست مضاعف * لفيف ناقص مهموز اجوف»
  (ṣaḥīḥ ast, mithāl ast, muḍāʿaf / lafīf, nāqiṣ, mahmūz, ajwaf); vowels ours, Arabic letters kept.

## Sections

11 sections, 46 passages (8–70 words): the six abwāb of the bare triliteral; the augmented triliteral in three
kinds (3 + 5 + 4 abwāb); the bare quadriliteral; six mulḥaq bi-daḥraja; three augmented quadriliterals; five
mulḥaq bi-tadaḥraja (with the note on ilḥāq); two mulḥaq bi-iḥranjama; the eight classes; the seven classes and
idghām.
