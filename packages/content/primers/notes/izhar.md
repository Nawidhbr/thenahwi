# Iẓhār al-asrār (Birgivī): transcription notes

## Source

- **Print**: «مجموعة في النحو تشتمل على ثلاث رسائل: الكافية لابن الحاجب، والإظهار والعوامل للبركوي», printed
  at **Maṭbaʿat al-Jawāʾib, Constantinople, 1302/1885** (the same print as `awamil`; see awamil/NOTES.md).
  Typeset, **without vowels**. The Iẓhār is on pp. 46–89 (p. 46 unnumbered, with the title «الإظهار» and the
  basmala); it ends «تم الإظهار بعون الله الغفار». Public domain.
- **Scan**: Princeton copy digitised by Google, on archive.org: https://archive.org/details/20260623_20260623_1051
  (PDF page = print page + 6, i.e. PDF pp. 52–95).
- **Second witness (consulted for doubtful places)**: the undated Ottoman lithograph of Kāfiya, Iẓhār and ʿAwāmil
  ("Nahiv Cümlesi"), https://archive.org/details/nahiv-cumlesi, Iẓhār on its pp. 45–73 (PDF spreads 24–38;
  spread s holds p. 2s−4 on the right and p. 2s−3 on the left), unvowelled, with marginal glosses.

## How it was checked

- Read from the page images at 200 dpi (doubtful words at 400 dpi), the text frame only; the marginal notes of the
  Jawāʾib print (marked «*» in the text) and the catchwords are left out. No digital text was used.
- **Vocalisation is ours** (the print has none), following the grammar the book itself teaches; a script checked
  that every consonant has its vowel, and that the lām of the article is right before sun and moon letters.
  Punctuation is ours; the print's parentheses around «القسم الأول», «فاللازم» etc. and its ornaments around the
  key words («فعل», «والعامل القياسي» …) are dropped.
- Conventions: a single noun cited after نحو is put in the genitive (نَحْوُ فَرْسَخٍ, نَحْوُ أُحَادَ …); sentences, verbs
  and particles are cited as they are (نَحْوُ: ضَرَبَ زَيْدٌ). Ordinals as in `awamil` (وَالْحَادِي عَشَرَ …).
  Old spellings normalised: واسئل → وَاسْأَلِ, ثمه → ثَمَّةَ.
- Qurʾān citations are vowelled as the Qurʾān, in the reading the rule needs (noted below).

## Readings to note

- p.50 «إلا مما بعد دخل ونزل وسكن» — both prints (lith p.48) read مما; kept.
- p.50 «زيد في الدار أي أكل» — both prints (lith p.48); kept.
- p.53 «ومن ثمه» normalised to ثَمَّةَ.
- p.53 «فإني أكرمه» vocalised فَإِنِّي (the point is that both readings are possible).
- p.53–54 «ولو كان غير متصرف أو شرط أو دعاء» — print writes شرط without the alif of the accusative; we read شَرْطًا أَوْ دُعَاءً.
- p.54 Qur'an 24:9 cited as «وَالْخَامِسَةَ أَنْ غَضِبَ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهَا» (the reading of Nāfiʿ, which the rule needs; Ḥafṣ reads أَنَّ غَضَبَ اللَّهِ).
- p.56 «ونعم غلام الرجل الزيدان»: the Jawāʾib print drops the alif of the dual; the lithograph (p. 51) has غلاما, which the rule (the makhṣūṣ agrees with the fāʿil) needs: we read غُلَامَا الرَّجُلِ.
- p.60 «والسابع اسم المضاف»: so the Jawāʾib print; the lithograph (p. 54) has «الاسم المضاف», which we give (as in the ʿAwāmil).
- p.66 «علمت قائم زيد»: the Jawāʾib print drops the hamza of the question; the lithograph (p. 58) has «اقائم»: we read عَلِمْتُ أَقَائِمٌ زَيْدٌ (taʿlīq by the istifhām).
- p.68 «نحو ضربت بحركات التاء» — the print gives the form unvowelled "with the vowels of the tāʾ" (ḍarabtu/ḍarabta/ḍarabti); we write ضَرَبْتَ.
- p.71 «نحو الله الهنا» — read اللَّهُ إِلَهُنَا.
- p.71 verse «اما القتال لا قتال لديكم» — the print gives only this hemistich and without the fāʾ of the usual citation (فَأَمَّا الْقِتَالُ); kept as printed.
- p.72 «ولا يلزم العامل» (maṣdar): both prints; we vowel وَلَا يَلْزَمُ الْعَامِلَ ("it need not stay next to its ʿāmil"); الْعَامِلُ ("the ʿāmil is not required") is also possible.
- p.73 «ويجوز تقديمه على عامله وتركه حذف عامله لقرينة»: the Jawāʾib print lacks a wāw; the lithograph (p. 62) has «وتركه وحذف عامله», which we give.
- p.74 «فلهذا لا يتقدم على فاعله» (tamyīz): as printed; one might expect «على عامله».
- p.76 «نحو قوله تعالى وكلا آتيناه» — as printed (the Qur'an has وَكُلًّا آتَيْنَا حُكْمًا وَعِلْمًا, 21:79); kept.
- p.76 «يريد الآخرة بجر الآخرة على قراءة» (8:67) — vowelled with the jarr the sentence describes.
- p.76–77 «أو ماضية غير متصرفة أو بمعناه فلا بد حينئذ من قد ظاهرة أو مقدرة» — wording as printed; the clause about qad is parenthetical.
- p.78–79 the feminine plural relatives: «اللواتي واللائي / واللاي واللاتي واللات واللوائي» as printed (اللاي = al-lāy; ثمه in «وأما ثمه وههنا» written ثَمَّةَ).
- p.83 «كضرب وشمر» (names on a verbal pattern): ضرب vowelled ضُرِبَ, the passive pattern being the one proper to verbs.
- p.85 «نحو ان زيد وهل زيد ومن زيد» (composite names whose second part is governed by an indeclinable word): the print has no alif after زيد, so not إنّ زيدًا; we read إِنْ زَيْدٌ.
- p.86 «مصطفوا القوم» and «ضاربوا القوم»: the print writes an alif after the wāw; we write مُصْطَفَوُ الْقَوْمِ and ضَارِبُو الْقَوْمِ.
- p.86 the pausal examples are given with sukūn (أَحْمَدْ، وَضَارِبَةْ، وَضَارِبَاتْ … زَيْدْ), since the rule is about the form in pause.
- p.89 «وحروف النداء يا وأيا وهيا وأي والهمزة ووا[و] مختص بالندبة»: the print reads «وواو»; we give the particle وَا.
- p.89 the print closes with «تم الإظهار بعون الله الغفار», which is left out of the text.

## Sections

4 sections: the opening (s1, heading the print's title «الإظهار»; the basmala is in p1) and the three abwāb with the
print's own headings (s2 الباب الأول في العامل, p2–p70; s3 الباب الثاني في المعمول, p71–p138; s4 الباب الثالث في
الإعراب, p139–p172). 172 passages, 18–81 words (median 48), about 8,200 words. Inside each bāb the print has no
further headings, only the ornamented key words, which open the passages.
