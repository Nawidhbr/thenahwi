# al-ʿAwāmil al-jadīda (Birgivī): transcription notes

## Source

- **Print**: «مجموعة في النحو تشتمل على ثلاث رسائل: الكافية لابن الحاجب، والإظهار والعوامل للبركوي»,
  printed with the licence of the Ministry of Education (نظارة المعارف) at **Maṭbaʿat al-Jawāʾib, Constantinople,
  1302** (colophon: «في غرة جمادى الأولى سنة ١٣٠٢», i.e. February 1885). Typeset, **without vowels**. al-ʿAwāmil is
  on pp. 90–99 (p. 90 unnumbered, with the title «العوامل» and the basmala). Public domain.
- **Scan**: Princeton University Library copy, digitised by Google, uploaded to archive.org:
  https://archive.org/details/20260623_20260623_1051 (PDF pp. 96–105 = pp. 90–99). The same print also holds the
  Iẓhār (useful for `izhar`).
- **Second witness (only consulted)**: an undated Ottoman lithograph of Kāfiya, Iẓhār and ʿAwāmil ("Nahiv Cümlesi"),
  https://archive.org/details/nahiv-cumlesi (PDF pp. 39–41), unvowelled, with marginal glosses.

## How it was checked

- Read from the page images (210 dpi), line by line; no digital text was used. Doubtful words were enlarged and,
  where the Jawāʾib print stays unclear, checked in the lithograph.
- **Vocalisation is ours** (the print has none), following the grammar the book itself teaches; a script checked
  that every consonant has its vowel. Punctuation is ours; the print's parentheses around «النوع الأول» etc. and
  «(والثاني)» etc. are dropped.

## Readings to note

- p. 91 (p7): «هَلَكَ الْعَالِمُونَ خَلَا الْعَامِلِ بِعَمَلِهِ»: the Jawāʾib print reads بعمله (enlarged); the lithograph has
  بعلمه («except the one who acts by his knowledge»), which is the better sense. We keep the print; the lesson may
  give the other reading.
- p. 92 (p7): «وَالتَّاسِعَ عَشَرَ كَيْ، نَحْوُ: كَيْمَهْ عَصَيْتَ»: the lithograph confirms كي … كيمه.
- p. 92 (p8): «إِنَّ اللَّهَ تَعَالَى عَالِمُ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ» (iḍāfa, no bāʾ): both prints.
- p. 94 (p18): «يُحِبُّ اللَّهُ تَعَالَى إِعْطَاءَهُ عَبْدَهُ فَقِيرًا دِرْهَمًا»: both prints have إعطاءه with the pronoun; we vowel
  it as the maṣdar with its agent (the pronoun), first object عَبْدَهُ, فَقِيرًا a ḥāl, second object دِرْهَمًا. Other copies
  may read «إِعْطَاءَ عَبْدِهِ فَقِيرًا دِرْهَمًا»; check with the teacher.
- p. 95 (p25): the example of the mafʿūl maʿahu is unclear in the Jawāʾib print (it seems to read «يفنى المال وسبق
  عملك», which does not show the rule). The lithograph reads clearly «يَفْنَى الْمَالُ وَتَبْقَى وَعَمَلَكَ» («wealth perishes,
  and you remain, together with your deeds»), which we give.
- p. 96 (p29): «أُطِيعُ اللَّهَ وَالرَّسُولَ» (the print writes اطيع with yāʾ, so first person, not the imperative أَطِعْ).
- p. 96 (p30): «أُبْغِضُ النَّاسَ مَنْ عَصَى اللَّهَ تَعَالَى مِنْهُمْ» (badal of part); vowels ours.
- p. 98 (p38): «نُحِبُّ أَنْ نَشْفَعَ وَلَمْ نُحْرَمْ»: نَشْفَعَ could also be read نُشَفَّعَ («be granted intercession»).
- p. 98 (p39): «نَدْعُو اللَّهَ تَعَالَى أَنْ يَعْفُوَنَا وَلَمْ يَرْمِنَا فِي النَّارِ» as printed (يعفونا); the sense is «that He pardon
  us, and He has not cast us into the Fire».
- Ordinals: «وَالْحَادِي عَشَرَ», «وَالثَّالِثَ عَشَرَ» …; «وَالْحَادِيَةَ عَشْرَةَ» … for the feminine list of jawāzim.
- The examples with لَعَلَّ in the dialect of ʿUqayl (p7) are vowelled «لَعَلَّ اللَّهِ» (with jarr), as the rule requires.

## Sections

4 sections: the opening (s1, no heading in the print except the title «العوامل», used as heading), the three
abwāb (s2–s4, the print's headings). 41 passages, 15–81 words.
