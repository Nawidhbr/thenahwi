# al-ʿAwāmil al-miʾa (Miʾat ʿāmil): transcription notes

## Source

- **Print**: *The Mi,ut Amil, and Shurhoo Mi,ut Amil; two elementary treatises on Arabic syntax*, translated with
  notes by Captain A. Lockett, Calcutta: printed by P. Pereira, 1814. The Arabic text of the matn («نسخه مائة عامل»)
  is printed at the end of the volume, separately paged (١)–(٥); the Arabic commentary (Sharḥ) follows it. The matn
  is **fully vocalised** in the print. Public domain.
- **Scan**: University of Toronto (Robarts) copy on archive.org: https://archive.org/details/miutamilshurhoom00abdauoft
  (PDF pp. 324–320 = pp. 1–5 of the Arabic text; the Arabic part is bound from the back).

## How it was checked

- Transcribed from the page images (150 dpi), lines enlarged where needed. No digital text was used. The Indian
  madrasa text of the Miʾat ʿāmil is the one printed here (the version attributed to al-Jurjānī read with the
  *Sharḥ Miʾat ʿāmil*).
- Script check: full vocalisation; ids p1–p21.
- The print has no headings; we use its own words: «مِائَةُ عَامِلٍ» (the title, for the introduction), «النَّوْعُ
  الْأَوَّلُ» … «النَّوْعُ الثَّالِثَ عَشَرَ», «وَالْقِيَاسِيَّةُ», «وَالْمَعْنَوِيَّةُ».

## Readings kept as printed

- p. 1: «مِنْهَا لَفْظِيَّةٌ وَمِنْهَا مَعْنَوِيَّةٌ … فَالسَّمَاعِيَّةُ مِنْهَا أَحَدٌ وَتِسْعُونَ عَامِلًا … وَالْمَعْنَوِيَّةُ مِنْهَا
  عَدَدَانِ» («two in number»).
- p. 1: «وَهِيَ سَبْعَةَ عَشَرَ حَرْفًا» (17 ḥurūf al-jarr in this text, ending حَاشَا وَخَلَا وَعَدَا).
- p. 2: «وَأَكَلْتُ السَّمَكَةَ حَتَّى رَأْسِهَا» (print: رَاسِهَا); «وَلَيْتَ الشَّبَابَ عَائِدٌ» (print: عَايِدٌ).
- p. 3: «وَلَامُ الْأَمْرِ وَلَاءُ النَّهْيِ» — the print writes the particle as a noun with hamza (لَاءُ); kept.
- p. 3: «وَأَيَّهُمْ تَضْرِبْ أَضْرِبْ» (accusative أيَّهم, as printed).
- p. 4: «وَمَا فَتِئَ زَيْدٌ قَائِمًا» — the example word is written قايما; could be read تائبا. Check.
- p. 4: «أَفْعَالُ الْمُقَارَبَةِ تَرْفَعُ اسْمًا وَاحِدًا», and «وَكَرَبَ زَيْدٌ يَخْرُجُ وَأَوْشَكَ زَيْدٌ يَجِيءُ» without أَنْ.
- p. 5: «وَوَجَدْتُ الْبَيْتَ رَهِينًا» as printed; «تَمَّتْ» at the end kept in the last passage.

## Sections

16 sections, 21 passages (20–55 words). The 100 governors: 91 samāʿī in 13 kinds (17 + 6 + 2 + 7 + 4 + 5 + 9 + 4
+ 9 + 13 + 4 + 4 + 7 = 91), 7 qiyāsī, 2 maʿnawī.
