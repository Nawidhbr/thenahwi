# Mīzān al-ṣarf: transcription notes

## Source

- **Print**: *Mīzān al-ṣarf*, Indian lithograph, Kanpur: Maṭbaʿ-i Razzāqī, 1905 (Digital Library of India, from the
  Maulana Azad Library, Aligarh Muslim University; scanned at 300 dpi): https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.433705
  ("Meezan Ul Sarf"). The matn is in large naskh in a ruled frame, with an interlinear Persian/Urdu gloss in small script
  under nearly every line and marginal notes. Mīzān al-ṣarf is on the print's pp. 3–24; the volume continues with other
  texts (Munshaʿib etc.), not transcribed here. Public domain.
- **PDF ↔ print pages**: PDF pp. 4–18 = pp. 3–17; PDF pp. 19–20 are **duplicate scans** of pp. 16–17; PDF pp. 21–27 =
  pp. 18–24.

## How it was checked

- Read from the page images (300 dpi, two pages at a time, the frame enlarged); only the large script of the matn.
  No digital text was used.
- **Persian** is given as printed, with the letters normalised: ک and گ (the lithograph writes گ without its second
  stroke), ی (Persian yāʾ) in Persian words; ي and ك inside the Arabic words and paradigms. Words are separated as in
  modern print where the lithograph runs them together (بزمانه، همبران، بعده kept as printed). Punctuation ours.
- **Arabic**: the opening ḥamdala, the phrases (أَسْعَدَكَ اللَّهُ …, قَلَّتْ حُرُوفُهُ أَوْ كَثُرَتْ) and all paradigms are
  vocalised as the print vowels them; missing vowels completed by us. The paradigm verb is فَعَلَ يَفْعُلُ (ḍamma on
  the ʿayn of the present, clearly printed: يَفْعُلُ، اُفْعُلْ), passive فُعِلَ يُفْعَلُ.
- Script checks: Arabic words never contain Persian ی/ک and Persian words never Arabic ي/ك; ids p1–p79.
- Tables: one passage per table, one row of the print per line, forms separated by «، ».

## Structure and headings

- The print's headings «بحث …» (each over its table) and «فصل» (inside the text, kept at the start of its passage)
  are kept. The first section is headed with the book's title «مِيزَانُ الصَّرْفِ» (ours; the print has only the basmala).
- **«فوائد نافعه»** (p. 5, on the māḍī qarīb, baʿīd, istimrārī, iḥtimālī and tamannī) is a boxed heading inside the
  matn frame in this print; it is not in every copy of the Mīzān. Kept as section s6; the lessons may treat it as an
  appendix.
- **«تتمه در بیان اسم ظرف و اسم آله و اسم تفضیل»** (pp. 23–24) is likewise printed in the frame after the passive
  participle and ends with «تمّت»; it is a later supplement found in Indian prints. Kept as the last sections.

## Readings to note

- p. 3: «و هر چه جز این سه چیز است متفرع است هم ازین سه»; «مگر بعارض».
- p. 4: the four patterns of the present: «يَفْعَلُ يَفْعِلُ يَفْعُلُ يُفَعْلِلُ» — the order on the cramped line is our
  reading; examples «يَضْرِبُ يَسْمَعُ يَكْرُمُ يُبَعْثِرُ» (past: «ضَرَبَ سَمِعَ كَرُمَ بَعْثَرَ»).
- p. 5: «كَانَ يَفْعَلُ» (fatḥa on the ʿayn here, as printed).
- p. 16: «چون لِيَدْعُ لِيَرْمِ لِيَخْشَ» — the first form is cramped; read لِيَدْعُ.
- pp. 17–18: the amr ghāʾib with the heavy nūn runs over the page break (row kept as the print breaks it).
- The light nūn tables (pp. 15, 18, 21) have eight forms for the lām al-taʾkīd (with لَتَفْعُلَنْ twice, as the print
  repeats it for the 3rd fem. and 2nd masc.), three for the amr/nahy of the present and five for the absent.
