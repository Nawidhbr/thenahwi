# al-Ājurrūmiyya: transcription notes

## Source

- **Print**: *Al-Adjrumiieh: The Arabic Text, with the Vowels, and an English Translation*, by the Rev. J. J. S.
  Perowne (Corpus Christi College), Cambridge: Macmillan and Co.; London: D. Nutt; Berlin: Asher & Co., 1852
  (printed by William M. Watts, London). Public domain.
- **Scan**: the copy of the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek (A.or. 1701 m), digitised by Google, on archive.org:
  https://archive.org/details/46-al-adjrumiieh (PDF `46 Al_Adjrumiieh.pdf`). The Arabic text is on pp. ٣–١٢
  (3–12; PDF pages 17–26); the English translation, which we do not use, is bound in the other direction.
- Perowne's preface: the Būlāq and Algiers editions were "printed without the vowels"; he "added the vowels
  throughout". So the vowels are the editor's, of 1852, and we keep them as printed.

## How it was checked

- Transcribed directly from the page images (rendered at 220 dpi), page by page; no digital text was used as a draft.
  Doubtful lines were cropped and enlarged and read again (listed below).
- A script checked that every consonant carries a vowel sign (or is a long vowel, the article's alif, or the lām
  before a sun letter), that passage ids run p1–p70, and that no tatweel, Persian kāf/yāʾ or waṣla is used.

## Conventions (ours, not the print's)

- Hamzat al-waṣl is written as a plain alif (the print has ٱ); where two sukūns meet we give the helping vowel the
  print leaves out (فَالِاسْمُ, مِنِ اسْمٍ, عَنِ الْعَوَامِلِ, بَاشَرَتِ النَّكِرَةَ).
- Alif maqṣūra is written ى (the print has dotted ي in إِلَي, عَلَي, مَتَي, أَنَّي, سِوَي, الْمُسْتَثْنَي); tanwīn
  al-fatḥ before the alif (زَيْدًا).
- إِعْلَمْ of the print (p. 11) is written اعْلَمْ; أَرَدتَّ (p. 9), printed with the tāʾ doubled by assimilation, is
  written أَرَدْتَ.
- Punctuation (، : .) is ours; the print has none except ۞ at the end of each chapter.
- The closing تَمَّ تَمَّ تَمَّ of p. 12 is left out.

## Where the print differs from the common text (kept as printed)

- p. 3, signs of the noun: no مُذْ and مُنْذُ among the particles (they come at the end of the book, p. 12).
- p. 4 (p9): «الَّذِي لَمْ يَتَّصِلْ بِآخِرِهِ شَيْءٌ وَإِذَا دَخَلَ عَلَيْهِ نَاصِبٌ» (most texts: «إِذَا دَخَلَ عَلَيْهِ نَاصِبٌ وَلَمْ
  يَتَّصِلْ بِآخِرِهِ شَيْءٌ»).
- p. 4 (p11): «وَأَمَّا حَذْفُ النُّونِ فَتَكُونُ» with tāʾ (confirmed enlarged).
- p. 5 (p18): «وَأَمَّا جَمْعُ الْمُذَكَّرِ السَّالِمِ فَيُرْفَعُ بِالْوَاوِ وَتُنْصَبُ وَتُخْفَضُ بِالْيَاءِ»: yāʾ then tāʾ, as printed
  (confirmed enlarged); a lesson may simply read يُنْصَبُ وَيُخْفَضُ.
- p. 5 (p21): «وَإِذًا» among the nawāṣib, with tanwīn, as printed.
- p. 6 (p25): no «قَامَ الرِّجَالُ» pair among the examples of the visible fāʿil.
- p. 7 (p35): «وَلَعَلَّ لِلتَّرَجِّي وَلِلتَّوَقُّعِ» (with a second lām; confirmed enlarged).
- p. 7–8 (p36): ẓanna and her sisters «تَنْصِبُ الِاسْمَ وَالْخَبَرَ» (most texts: الْمُبْتَدَأَ وَالْخَبَرَ).
- p. 8 (p41): the examples of ʿaṭf stop at «وَمَرَرْتُ بِزَيْدٍ وَعَمْرٍو» (no example with jazm).
- p. 9 (p45): «الْمَنْصُوبَاتُ خَمْسَةَ عَشَرَ», but the list has fourteen items: the print does not name the two
  objects of ẓanna (مَفْعُولَا ظَنَّ وَأَخَوَاتِهَا) that other texts list. Kept as printed; the lesson should say so.
- p. 9 (p44): «فَغَلِطْتَ»: the print's vowel on the lām is not clear (it may be fatḥa); we give the usual kasra.
- p. 11 (p60): «وَأَمَّا الْمُسْتَثْنَى بِغَيْرٍ ... مَجْرُورٌ لَا غَيْرُ» without the fāʾ of the answer to أَمَّا, as printed.
- p. 11 (p57): the print vocalises the list «إِلَّا وَغَيْرُ وَسِوَى وَسُوَى وَسَوَاءُ» without tanwīn; kept.
- p. 12 (p66): the mafʿūl maʿahu is «لِبَيَانِ مَنْ فُعِلَ مَعَهُ الْفِعْلُ», as printed.
- p. 12 (p69): the ḥurūf al-qasam are followed directly by «وَبِمُذْ وَمُنْذُ».

## Left out: the editor's two additions in square brackets

- p. 11, bāb lā: «وَجَبَ الرَّفْعُ [وَالتَّنْوِينُ]» — the bracketed word is Perowne's addition (the common text has it);
  left out of p62.
- p. 12, makhfūḍāt: «وَالتَّاءِ [وَبِوَاوِ رُبَّ] وَبِمُذْ وَمُنْذُ» — again Perowne's bracket (the common text has
  «وَبِوَاوِ رُبَّ»); left out of p69. The lessons may mention both as found in other copies.

## Uncertain readings

- p. 10 (p51): after «بِتَقْدِيرِ فِي نَحْوُ» the vowel of «الْيَوْمَ» is not clear in the scan (kasra or fatḥa); we give
  fatḥa, like the rest of the list (وَغُدْوَةً, وَبُكْرَةً … all with tanwīn al-fatḥ).
- p. 3 (p1): «لِمَعْنًى»: the print shows a single fatḥa on the nūn; we write the tanwīn.

## Sections

26 chapters (s1–s26), the print's headings; 70 passages, 8–47 words each.
