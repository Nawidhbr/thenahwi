## Title (30/30)

The Nahwi: learn ṣarf and naḥw

## Short description (77/80)

Arabic grammar as the madrasa teaches it: ṣarf first, then naḥw, book by book

## Full description

The Nahwi teaches ṣarf and naḥw, Arabic morphology and syntax, the way the madrasa teaches them: ṣarf first, then naḥw, book after book.

• Three madrasa paths (Indian, Ottoman and North-African), starting from al-Mabādiʾ, the first terms of both sciences, free for everyone with an account.
• A conjugation tool: any sound triliteral verb in its bāb and its augmented forms, past and present in all fourteen persons, active and passive, with the imperative, the participles and the maṣdar.
• Every lesson ends with a short test; the next lesson opens with two stars.
• Tikrār: reviews on a schedule, so that what you learned stays, with exercises generated from the verbs you know.
• Every Arabic word fully vocalised, with its transliteration.
• A free encyclopedia of the handbooks (al-Kāfiya, al-Shāfiya, al-ʿIzzī, the Alfiyya, al-Ājurrūmiyya, Qaṭr al-nadā), their commentaries and the grammarians who wrote them.
• Your progress book by book, a daily goal, and a certificate for every book you complete.
• No advertisements; once installed, the lessons work offline.

Every explanation and test is a draft until a qualified teacher of naḥw and ṣarf has reviewed it, and each lesson says whether it has been reviewed.
