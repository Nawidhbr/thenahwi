## Standing

| Decision | Why |
| --- | --- |
| English first; translations in a separate pass | English is the main audience |
| Ṣarf and naḥw only; balāgha later only as their extension, as a bonus text; URLs stay `/<locale>/terms/<slug>/` | no other subjects are planned |
| Never reproduce or translate from a modern edition under copyright; show only public-domain prints | the law, and the site's trust |
| Every explanation is a draft until a qualified teacher has reviewed it, and says so (once, on the About page) | the teaching must be checked by a teacher |
